Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Last updated: October 1, 2026

The short version

  • Your scans, PDFs and recognized text are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no login, and we run no servers that receive your documents.
  • Scanning, OCR, compression and PDF tools work on your device and offline.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and the app shows no ads.
  • A document leaves the app only when you choose to share, save, export or print it.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the ScanKaro – PDF Scanner & OCR mobile application (package com.scanzo.app.scanzo_pdf_scanner), referred to as “ScanKaro”, “the app”, “we”, “us” or “our”, handles information when you use it.

By installing or using ScanKaro, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. ScanKaro has no user accounts, does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Content you create in the app is stored locally on your device and never sent to us:

  • Documents: scanned pages, imported images and PDFs, merged or compressed files, and signed documents.
  • Recognized text: text extracted from your documents using on-device OCR, which is used for in-app search and copying.
  • Signatures: signatures you draw or import to sign PDFs.
  • QR codes: content you scan or generate.
  • App settings: preferences such as theme, view mode and app lock settings.

3. How your information is processed

All core processing happens on your device:

  • Edge detection, cropping, filters and image enhancement
  • PDF creation, merging, compression and signing
  • Text recognition (OCR) and document search
  • Text-to-speech, which uses your device’s built-in speech engine

The app does not need an internet connection for these features, and we do not receive copies of your documents.

4. Device permissions

ScanKaro requests only the permissions its features need. You can revoke them anytime in your device settings.

PermissionWhy it is used
CameraUsed to scan documents. Scanning runs through Google’s document scanner or your device’s camera app, and images are returned only to ScanKaro.
Photos & filesUsed only when you import images or PDFs, or save and export documents. On newer Android versions, the system photo picker is used, so the app never gets access to your whole gallery.

5. Third-party services

ScanKaro uses the following on-device components provided by Google:

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition: performs OCR on your device.
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner: runs inside Google Play services to capture and crop documents.

Your document images and text are processed on your device by these components and are not uploaded by ScanKaro. Google Play services may independently collect limited diagnostic and usage information under Google’s own terms. See the ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

When you share, print or export a document, it is passed to the app or service you choose, such as email, a messaging app, cloud storage or a printer. That service’s own privacy policy then applies.

6. Data storage and security

  • Documents are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access.
  • Hidden Vault and App lock credentials are protected with your device’s secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Android cloud backup of app data is turned off, so your documents are not copied to backup services automatically.

No method of storage is completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock, and keep your own backups of important documents.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are fully in control of it:

  • When you delete a document, it moves to the Recycle bin. It is permanently erased after 30 days or when you empty the bin.
  • You can export a copy of your library at any time from Settings › Back up library. You are responsible for storing that backup file securely.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything ScanKaro stored on your device.

Since we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover deleted documents for you.

8. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information or documents with anyone. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or training models.

9. Children’s privacy

ScanKaro is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information, no such data is held by us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. These can include rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data.

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our systems, you can exercise these rights directly in the app by viewing, editing, exporting or deleting your documents. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we will also announce it in the app or on its store listing.
